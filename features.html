Navigation & Cockpit
Vehicle Arrow Tap
Cycles the map basemap between Dark Matter (Night) and Positron (High-Contrast Neutral Day). Automatically syncs the Speed Sign tile theme.
Compass Rose Tap
Toggles map tracking between Heading-Up (vehicle locked lower-third, pointing forward) and North-Up (classic fixed orientation).
Fullscreen Expander Tap
Locks the app into immersive browser fullscreen mode, hiding top bars and search interfaces.
Telemetry & Overlays
Top-Left Telemetry Badge Tap
Cycles the display data across 3 real-time GPS modes: Heading (Degrees + Cardinal) → Altitude (Feet) → GPS Accuracy (± Feet).
Highway Shield Detection Auto
Renders real-time Interstate Shields (lower-left) and State/Numbered Route Badges (lower-right) with automatic directional snapping (North, South, East, West).
Speed Sign Tile Tap
Instantly switches speed calculation and unit display between MPH and KM/H.
Location & Weather
Location Card Short Tap
Copies a direct Google Maps coordinate pin link (`https://maps.google.com/?q=...`) to your clipboard with a confirmation toast.
About & Branding Hold 600ms
Long-pressing the top Location Card opens the developer and app information modal.
Temperature Unit Short Tap
Switches current temperature readout between Fahrenheit (°F) and Celsius (°C).
Weather Refresh Trigger Hold 500ms
Opens the update interval manager to configure time refresh triggers (10m, 20m, 30m, 60m) and distance thresholds (15mi, 30mi, 50mi, Off).
System & Performance
Screen Wake Lock Auto
Background heartbeat continuously preserves screen-on state through window minimizes, app switching, and fullscreen toggles.
Ghost Number Stripper Auto
Automatically suppresses false residential house numbers and highway segment markers while traveling on designated expressways and interstates.
